SAFETY INFORMATION

Safety information

  • Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and high temperatures.
  • Do not scratch or puncture the product with sharp objects.
  • Clean and disinfect the product before and after use, then store it completely dry.
  • Do not use in hot, humid places or in salt water or seawater. This may cause damage.
  • Do not soak the product for long periods or use steam cleaning.
  • The product is splash resistant, but it is not designed for underwater use or strong water flow.
  • Do not submerge or use the product in water deeper than 30 cm.
  • Do not use when you feel unwell, tired, or after drinking alcohol.
  • Do not drop, strike, or apply excessive force to the product. This may cause internal damage.
  • Do not use organic solvents, thinner, alcohol, bleach, boiling water, or similar cleaning agents.
  • If you feel unwell while using the product, stop immediately and consult a doctor.
  • If the product behaves abnormally, turn it off immediately.

For users aged 18 and over only. For hygiene reasons, do not share the product.
